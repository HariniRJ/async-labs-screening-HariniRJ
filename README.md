# Async Labs Screening – Smart Workspace Display

## Overview
A conversion-focused landing page concept for Async Labs’ 7.5-inch smart workspace display, designed around college students who study at a desk.

## Concepts
- Concept 1 :“Keep what matters in sight.”
- Concept 2 :“Your study space, at a glance.” — selected as the final direction.

## Final Direction
The final concept positions the display as a personalized study companion that keeps classes, deadlines, tasks and focus sessions visible without needing to switch between apps.

## Supporting Section
“Everything you need, without another tab.” reinforces the core value through Calendar, Tasks and Focus features.

## Mobile
A responsive mobile adaptation of the selected Concept 2 hero is included.

## Tools
Claude was used for visual concept generation and iteration. Figma was used during the initial exploration.

## Time
Approximately 4 hours.
